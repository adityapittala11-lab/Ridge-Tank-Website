Sponsor terms & refunds

Last updated November 20, 2026

Ridge Tank is a student program at Mountain Ridge High School, run under the school's FBLA chapter. These are the plain-language rules for sponsoring us.

What you pay for

  • Hoodie sponsor ($333): your name or logo on the back of our club hoodies, plus the perks listed on the sponsor page.
  • Meeting sponsor: your name on that meeting's posters and screen, plus the perks listed on the sponsor page.
  • Spots are limited. If a spot sells out, the site won't let you pay for it.

How payment works

  • You pay by card on our site. Stripe handles the payment. We never see or store your card number.
  • Sponsorships pay for club costs like hoodies, snacks and prizes. They are not tax-deductible donations.
  • Our adviser, Mr. Hintz, can confirm anything about the club.

Refunds

  • Charged twice or by mistake: full refund, always.
  • Changed your mind: full refund within 7 days of paying, as long as your name or logo hasn't been sent to print or shown at a meeting yet.
  • We can't deliver: if the hoodie isn't made or your meeting is canceled and not rescheduled, full refund.
  • After your name is printed or shown: no refund, since the spot has been used.
  • To ask for a refund, email ridgetank.club@gmail.com. Refunds go back to the same card through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Your logo

  • By sending us your logo, you let us use it for your sponsorship: hoodies, posters, our site and our Instagram.
  • Want it taken off the site or Instagram later? Email us and we'll remove it. Printed hoodies can't be changed.
  • We're a school program, so we may turn down a sponsor that isn't a fit for students. If we do, you get a full refund.

Questions

Email ridgetank.club@gmail.com. See also our privacy page.
