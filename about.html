Ridge Tank (also written RidgeTank) is the student startup program at Mountain Ridge High School in Glendale, Arizona. Ridge Tank hoodies are official merchandise of this school program. Ridge Tank is a student club and is not affiliated with ABC’s Shark Tank.

Somewhere at Mountain Ridge right now, a student has an idea they haven't said out loud yet. Ridge Tank is the room where they finally say it, and the room says yes.

Most high school clubs ask you to sit and listen. Ridge Tank asks you to stand up, pitch, get judged, and walk out with something you built. It's a Shark Tank-style startup program at Mountain Ridge High School in Glendale, Arizona, run with the school's FBLA chapter. Anyone can walk in. It's free. You don't need a plan, a product, or permission. You need an idea and the nerve to say it.

The most common thing students say after a meeting: "I didn't know someone my age could actually do this."

That feeling is the point. Ridge Tank turns "someday" into "this week." You stop being the person who has ideas and become the person who tests them, sells them, and shows them to a room full of people who are watching.

Meetings: where nerves turn into momentum

Meetings run one to two times a month, and every one has a guest speaker. You'll leave with a story you can repeat, a skill you can use, and a little more courage than you walked in with.

  • First meeting: Wed Oct 21, 2026, 2:20 PM, Media Center. Over 40 students came, and the room was loud with questions.
  • Second meeting: Thu Nov 19, 2026, Media Center. Phase 01: Spark kickoff. 60+ students came. Shark Tank founder Sabin Lomac spoke by video, then teams pitched real ideas and our student Sharks picked the top three.
  • December meeting: date to be announced. Keep your calendar open.

What happens in the room

A guest who did itA founder talks about how they started, what went wrong, and what they'd do again. Then you get to ask the questions you were scared to ask.
The 5-Second TestTeams of 2 or 3 pitch a real idea in five seconds. If a stranger can't get it fast, it isn't clear yet. You'll learn to make your idea land.
Student SharksJudges score every pitch out of 10 and give one honest line. Getting judged by peers is scary. It's also how you get better, fast.
Prizes and a teaserTop teams take home gift cards. A free giveaway makes it fun. The teaser at the end makes you want to come back.

The school year: a story with a finish line

Ridge Tank runs in four phases. Each one moves you from a feeling to a result, and each ends with a moment you'll remember.

PhaseDatesWhat you do, and how it feels
SparkNow to Dec 2, 2026Find a real problem and pick your idea. The feeling: curiosity, then the first spark of "I could build this."
BuildDec 2, 2026 to Feb 10, 2027Make the smallest version that works, test it, and launch it. The feeling: the pressure of making it real.
SellFeb 10 to Apr 7, 2027Make real sales and reach your first ten customers. The feeling: the rush when someone pays for what you made.
GrowApr 7 to May 12, 2027Grow what's working and prepare your final pitch. The feeling: pride in what you built.
  • Demo Day: Wed Feb 10, 2027. The first time many students show the world what they built.
  • Grand Finale: Wed May 12, 2027. The biggest night of the year, and the one you'll tell people about.

Questions

Is Ridge Tank free to join?

Yes. Any Mountain Ridge High School student can walk into a meeting. No sign-up is needed to attend.

Where and when do meetings happen?

Meetings are held at Mountain Ridge High School in the Media Center, about one to two times a month. The next one is in December 2026. The date will be announced on this site and our Instagram.

Who runs Ridge Tank?

Ridge Tank is led by its student officers, with Aditya Pittala as President and Mr. Hintz as adviser. It runs with the school's FBLA chapter.

Do I need a business idea to come?

No. Come to listen, meet people, and see what's possible. The program helps you find an idea and turn it into something real.

When is Demo Day?

Demo Day is Wednesday, February 10, 2027. The Grand Finale is Wednesday, May 12, 2027.
