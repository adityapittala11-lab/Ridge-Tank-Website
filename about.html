Ridge Tank is where Mountain Ridge students stop talking about starting a business and start one. Students pitch real ideas to a room of their peers, get judged by student Sharks, and build something that could actually exist.

Ridge Tank is a Shark Tank-style startup program at Mountain Ridge High School in Glendale, Arizona. It runs with the school's FBLA chapter, and it's built around one idea: high school students can build real businesses, and they should get the chance to try.

Anyone at Mountain Ridge can join. There's no cost and no sign-up needed to walk into a meeting. The program grows through the school year, from first idea (Spark) to first customers (Sell) to a final pitch at the Grand Finale.

What happens in the room

Guest speakerA founder or leader talks about how they started, what went wrong, and what they'd do again. Then students ask questions.
5-Second TestTeams of 2 or 3 pitch a real idea in five seconds. Any judge should be able to understand it right away.
Student SharksStudent judges score each pitch out of 10 and add a one-line comment. Sharks are picked through an application.
Prizes and raffleTop teams take home gift cards. There's a raffle and a teaser for the next meeting at the end.

The school year

Ridge Tank runs in four phases. Each one ends with a milestone students work toward.

PhaseDatesWhat students do
SparkNow to Dec 2, 2026Find a real problem, pick an idea, and talk to the people who have it.
BuildDec 2, 2026 to Feb 10, 2027Build the smallest version that works, test it, and launch it.
SellFeb 10 to Apr 7, 2027Make real sales and reach the first ten customers.

Questions

Is Ridge Tank free to join?

Yes. Any Mountain Ridge High School student can walk into a meeting. No sign-up is needed to attend.

Where and when do meetings happen?

Meetings are held at Mountain Ridge High School in the Media Center, about one to two times a month. The next one is Thu Nov 19, 2026, at 2:25 PM Arizona time.

Who runs Ridge Tank?

Ridge Tank is led by its student officers, with Aditya Pittala as President and Mr. Hintz as adviser. It runs with the school's FBLA chapter.

Do I need a business idea to come?

No. Come to learn, listen, and meet people. The program is built to help you find an idea and turn it into something real.

When is Demo Day?

Demo Day is Wednesday, February 10, 2027. The Grand Finale is Wednesday, May 12, 2027.
