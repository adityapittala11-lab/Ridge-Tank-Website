What Ridge Tank is

Ridge Tank is a student-run startup program at Mountain Ridge High School in Glendale, Arizona. It works like Shark Tank. Students pitch real ideas, get judged by student Sharks, and build real businesses. It runs with the school's FBLA chapter. Any Mountain Ridge student can walk into a meeting. Membership is free.

  • School: Mountain Ridge High School, Glendale, Arizona
  • Season: 2026-2027
  • President: Aditya Pittala. Adviser: Mr. Hintz.
  • Officers: Kavin (Vice President), Navya (Communications)

Meetings

  • First meeting: Wed Oct 21, 2026, 2:20 PM, Media Center. 40+ students came.
  • Next meeting: Thu Nov 19, 2026, 2:25 PM, Media Center. Phase 01: Spark kickoff. The date may change, so check the site.
  • Meetings happen 1 to 2 times a month. Every meeting has a guest speaker.
  • 5-Second Test: teams of 2-3 pitch a real idea. Student Sharks score it out of 10 with a one-line comment.
  • Past guest: Chaitanya NK, co-founder and CEO of Track3D (AI software for construction), joined by Zoom for Q&A on Oct 21, 2026.

Points and leaderboard

  • Attendance: 100 points per meeting.
  • Pitch points: 300 for 1st, 200 for 2nd, 100 for 3rd, 50 for pitching.
  • The leaderboard closes Dec 31. First place wins iFLY tickets.
