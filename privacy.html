Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Ridge Tank is a student program at Mountain Ridge High School. This site tracks attendance, Bites (the program's points), badges, and a members-only chat. Here's what it stores and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, grade, and email address. If you sign in with Google, we get your name and email from Google. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Program activity: which meetings you checked in to, pitch results, Bites you invested in other members, bounties you claimed (and the note or link you sent with them), your Academy progress, badges, and your Bites.
  • Your phone number: asked for when you join, and whether you're on WhatsApp. Officers use it to text or WhatsApp you program updates, like meeting reminders. They ask before adding anyone to a group chat. You can change or remove it any time on your profile.
  • AI sharks: when you practice with the AI sharks or describe an idea to The Sharks, what you type in that round is sent to an AI service (Groq) so the sharks can answer. We don't send your name, email, or member ID with it. Please don't type personal details like phone numbers or addresses into a round.
  • Chat: messages you post in the program chat.

Who can see it

  • When you sign up or log in we keep a random ID for your browser and a scrambled (one-way) version of your network address. Only officers can see matches, and only to spot one person using two accounts. We never store your actual network address.
  • Signed-in members can see everyone's name, Bites, and tier on the leaderboard, and messages in the program chat.
  • Only you can see your own check-in history and badge progress.
  • Program officers can see member details (including emails, phone numbers, and login codes) so they can run check-in and send updates. Your phone number is never shown to other members.
  • Announcements and the calendar are visible to every signed-in member.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone outside the program.
  • We don't show ads or use tracking cookies. The site only saves what it needs in your browser to keep you logged in.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a private database that only this site can reach, and is locked down so people can only read what the rules above allow.

Sponsors and payments

Businesses that sponsor Ridge Tank give us their business name, a contact name, an email address and a logo. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers. We keep a record of what each sponsor paid and which plan they chose so we can deliver what they paid for and send receipts. Sponsor names and logos appear on our posters, hoodies, website and Instagram only after the sponsor signs up for that.

Students under 18

Ridge Tank is a high school program, so most members are under 18. We collect only what we need to run meetings and updates, we don't sell it, and a parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete their student's information at any time by emailing ridgetank.club@gmail.com.

Questions

Email ridgetank.club@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

Ask any program officer to delete your account. We'll remove your profile, phone number, check-ins, badges, bounty claims, Academy progress, and chat messages.
