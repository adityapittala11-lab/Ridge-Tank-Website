Privacy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Ridge Tank is a student program at Mountain Ridge High School. This site tracks attendance, Bites (the program's points), badges, and a members-only chat. Here's what it stores and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, grade, and email address. If you sign in with Google, we get your name and email from Google. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Program activity: which meetings you checked in to, pitch results, Bites you invested in other members and any bounties you claimed, badges, and your Bites.
  • Your card: the ID number built into your Ridge Tank card, so tapping it can check you in.
  • Chat: messages you post in the program chat.

Who can see it

  • Signed-in members can see everyone's name, Bites, and tier on the leaderboard, and messages in the program chat.
  • Only you can see your own check-in history and badge progress.
  • Program officers can see member details (including emails and card IDs) so they can run check-in and make cards.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone outside the program.
  • We don't show ads or use tracking cookies. The site only saves what it needs in your browser to keep you logged in.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a database service, and is locked down so people can only read what the rules above allow.

Deleting your data

Ask any program officer to delete your account. We'll remove your profile, check-ins, badges, and chat messages.
